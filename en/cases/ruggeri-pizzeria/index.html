Ruggeri Pizzería

A concept project and my final project: the website of an artisan pizzeria with a menu, build-your-own pizza and online ordering.

The project

Project
Ruggeri Pizzería · own concept, not a client
Type
Website with catalog and cart

The challenge

Show how a pizzeria can sell online: «artisan pizzas made with love and respect for tradition, with fresh ingredients and sourdough fermented for 48 hours», with clear prices and ordering in a few taps.
