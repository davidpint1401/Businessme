Rucha: 556,010 people reached in one month

Ten months of campaigns for the hair tools brand Rucha and its training academy, Rucha Academy.

The client

Client
Rucha (@rucha.ve) and Rucha Academy
Industry
Professional hair tools and courses for stylists
Reach
All of Venezuela, focused on Lara, Zulia, Capital District and Carabobo
Service
Monthly Meta Ads management and monthly report
Period
September 2025 to June 2026

The challenge

Make Rucha's star products, such as the Turbo Styler Pro Digital and the Onyx line, known nationwide, and fill Rucha Academy's courses and workshops with interested students.

What I did

  1. 1

    Step 1: Video awareness

    ThruPlay view campaigns so the whole country gets to know the star products at the lowest possible cost.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Traffic and conversations

    Profile visit campaigns to gain followers, and DM and WhatsApp message campaigns to turn interest into students and buyers.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Monthly report and adjustments

    Analysis by ad, age, gender and region. Each report ends with recommendations for the next month.

Key results

  • 556,010 people reached in one month Rucha Academy · Oct 2025
  • 34,466 profile visits at $0.029 Rucha Academy · Oct 2025
  • 1.09 M ThruPlay views at $0.00046 Rucha · Mar–Apr 2026
  • $0.013 per profile visit, the lowest cost Rucha · Apr–May 2026 · 13,757 visits
  • +1,921 new followers in one month Rucha · Apr–May 2026
  • 824 DM conversations at $0.23 Rucha Academy · Mar–Apr 2026
  • 766 conversations at $0.24 Rucha · Dec 2025 · Onyx campaign
  • 36 s average watch time per video Rucha · Turbo Styler Pro Digital
