Guaroshop: 3,875 conversations in one month

3,875 people messaged the professional barber store on WhatsApp, at $0.24 per conversation, all costs included.

The client

Client
Guaroshop · @guaroshop2023
Industry
Professional barber supply store
Cities
Barquisimeto and Acarigua, Venezuela
Service
Monthly Meta Ads management
Period
23 September to 26 October 2026

The challenge

Guaroshop sells in two cities. It needed more WhatsApp messages in Barquisimeto at a lower cost, and to become known in Acarigua so people there would message too.

What I did

  1. 1

    Step 1: 6 campaigns, one goal each

    Instagram traffic, WhatsApp messages in each city, brand awareness in Acarigua and two campaigns for the LIZZE product.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Product videos as ads

    Videos of products such as Kemei and LIZZE, and the Cashea payment announcement. I measured how many messages each video brought in and at what cost.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Monthly report by city

    Cost per message in each city, comparison with the previous month and adjustments for the next one.

Results

  • 3,875 WhatsApp conversations $0.24 per conversation
  • $913 spent across 6 campaigns $913.43 in total
  • 13 sales recorded tagged in WhatsApp Business
  • 2,398 new followers on Instagram
  • 11,870 profile visits at $0.017 each
  • −28% cost per message in Barquisimeto vs. previous month
  • −56% cost per message in Acarigua vs. previous month
  • 403,037 people reached in Acarigua with $141.48
